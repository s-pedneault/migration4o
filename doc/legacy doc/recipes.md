# Recipes

Processing DB4O databases is a tricky process that comes with several intricacies that have been learned over time. Unfortunately, because of these sometimes-unintuitive requirements or techniques, AI often fails to understand the recognize that and reinvents the wheel. Thus we focus our efforts on developing unified reusable recipes for various tasks, so not to continuously reinvent everything.
